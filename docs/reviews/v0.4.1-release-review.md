# Release review: v0.4.1 (follow-up fixes)

**Reviewer:** GPT-6.2 Astra, high effort, read-only, from `ace6728` (v0.4.0).

## Round 1 (`839b524`)

Five should-fixes, all fixed in `d4cbbc0`.

| # | Severity | Finding | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Should-fix | The staging lease was created with `open` and locked with a separate `flock`, so a second launch could unlink it in between. | Fixed: leases are opened with `O_EXLOCK \| O_NONBLOCK`, and staging uses `O_CREAT \| O_EXCL`. |
| 2 | Should-fix | v0.4.0 and v0.4.1 copies in one folder could delete each other's staged update. | Fixed in round 3: v0.4.1 stages under `.OpenToolbox-staged-` and leaves v0.4.0's `.OpenToolbox-update-` copies alone. |
| 3 | Should-fix | A recording that couldn't leave its capture folder was reported as "kept below". | Fixed: the message shows the path whenever the recording stays where it was captured. |
| 4 | Should-fix | A relaunch failure after a successful install was reported as "wasn't installed". | Fixed: new installed-and-waiting-to-reopen state. |
| 5 | Should-fix | A failed interactive install kept its staging lease until quit. | Fixed: `abandon(_:)` releases it. |

## Round 2 (`d3e6acd`)

Confirmed round 1 #1 and #3–5, #2 partial (a seven-day age rule for legacy copies). Raised:

| # | Severity | Finding | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Blocker | `install` didn't check the incoming bundle at its final path after the swap (also present in v0.4.0). | Fixed in `3b3b828`: the incoming app is verified at the installed path and the previous app is put back on mismatch; `installPutsTheOldAppBackWhenTheUpdateChangesDuringTheSwap`. |
| 2 | Should-fix | `discard` kept the lease when the staged copy was missing. | Fixed: the lease is released in `defer`. |
| 3 | Should-fix | A quit cancelled by a recording left a relaunch helper waiting for the next quit. | Fixed: the helper starts in `applicationWillTerminate`, and a cancelled quit forgets it. |
| 4 | Should-fix | The README promised recovery for temporary-folder recordings and unconditional notifications. | Fixed in `59f6c0f` (README by Gemini 4 Neon). |
| 5 | Nit | The README said the Disk Speed Test file is "always cleaned up". | Fixed in `59f6c0f`. |

`87f0db2` also makes `EngineStore.tryLock` retry `EINTR` instead of reporting the lock busy, after `uninstallRemovesEverything` failed once on the Mac mini.

## Round 3 (`59f6c0f`)

Confirmed round 2 #1–5. Raised three should-fixes, all fixed in `7d11ccd`:

1. Legacy cleanup still raced a v0.4.0 copy starting during cleanup. Fixed: legacy staged copies are never removed by v0.4.1.
2. Another update check erased the installed-and-waiting state after a cancelled quit. Fixed: `checkNow` keeps it; `anInstalledUpdateWaitingForRelaunchSurvivesALaterCheck`.
3. A FIFO in place of the staged executable could block Restart Now or Quit. Fixed: bundle files are opened with `O_NONBLOCK \| O_NOFOLLOW` and accepted only as regular files; `aPipeInPlaceOfABundleFileIsRefusedWithoutWaiting`.

## Round 4 (`7d11ccd`)

Confirmed all round 3 findings and round 1 #2 resolved. One nit (the FIFO test would hang rather than fail if the regression returned), not changed.
